How is the score worked out?

Throughput (TP) combines speed and accuracy into one number (ISO 9241-9): how many bits of target information you get through per second. Rushing and landing off-centre doesn't help, because scattered selections make the targets count as smaller.

  • Per technique: your mean throughput over every target ring in that technique.
  • Overall: your mean throughput across the techniques. People who finished all of them are ranked first.
  • Time is the mean movement time per selection; timeouts are selections that hit the time limit.
